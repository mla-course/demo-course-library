** {{{rb(初めての挨拶,Hajimete no aisatsu,First Greetings)}}}
:PROPERTIES:
:HTML_CONTAINER_CLASS: text
:END:

A short dialogue to practice greetings, with pronunciation and
translation shown as ruby annotations.

Notice that Japanese greetings change with the time of day — {{{rb(おはよう,ohayou,good morning)}}}
in the morning, {{{rb(こんにちは,konnichiwa,hello)}}} for the rest of the day.

#+begin_src hanyu2
*Tanaka: おはようございます。お名前は何ですか。
*Tanaka: Ohayou gozaimasu. Onamae wa nan desu ka.
*Tanaka: Good morning. What is your name?

*Anna: 私の名前はアンナです。よろしくお願いします。
*Anna: Watashi no namae wa Anna desu. Yoroshiku onegaishimasu.
*Anna: My name is Anna. Nice to meet you.

*Tanaka: はじめまして、アンナさん。
*Tanaka: Hajimemashite, Anna-san.
*Tanaka: Nice to meet you, Anna.
#+end_src

The word {{{rb(すみません,sumimasen,excuse me / sorry)}}} is one of
the most useful phrases in Japanese — it works both as an apology and
as a polite way to get someone's
attention. {{{rb(ありがとう,arigatou,thank you)}}}
and {{{rb(すみません,sumimasen,excuse me / sorry)}}} will come up in
almost every conversation you have.

** {{{rb(教室にて,Kyoushitsu nite,In the Classroom)}}}
:PROPERTIES:
:HTML_CONTAINER_CLASS: text
:END:

A second dialogue introduces the classroom vocabulary from this lesson: {{{rb(教室,kyoushitsu,classroom)}}}, {{{rb(学生,gakusei,student)}}}, {{{rb(先生,sensei,teacher)}}}, and {{{rb(水,mizu,water)}}}.

#+begin_src hanyu2
*Sensei: 皆さん、こんにちは。ここは教室です。私は先生です。
*Sensei: Minasan, konnichiwa. Koko wa kyoushitsu desu. Watashi wa sensei desu.
*Sensei: Everyone, hello. This is the classroom. I am the teacher.

*Anna: こんにちは、先生。私は学生です。
*Anna: Konnichiwa, sensei. Watashi wa gakusei desu.
*Anna: Hello, teacher. I am a student.

*Sensei: すみません、水を飲んでもいいですか。
*Sensei: Sumimasen, mizu o nondemo ii desu ka.
*Sensei: Excuse me, may I drink some water?

*Anna: はい、どうぞ。
*Anna: Hai, douzo.
*Anna: Yes, please go ahead.
#+end_src

Notice how {{{rb(食べる,taberu,to eat)}}} and other verbs will appear in later lessons once you are comfortable with these basic greetings and classroom words.
